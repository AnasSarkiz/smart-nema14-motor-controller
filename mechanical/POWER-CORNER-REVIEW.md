# Power disconnect and regeneration review — revision 15

Analytical screens only. Routing is disabled; no hardware test or fabrication readiness is claimed. Exact calculation inputs and results are in evidence/rev-0.0.15-alpha.0/POWER-CORNER-SCREEN.json.

TPS26600RHFR/C2155767 replaces TPS25947. OUT is rated independently to 60 V operating / 62 V absolute, so the previous input-relative limit on USB removal is resolved. The complete system remains limited by TMC2209's 29 V operating maximum and lower component/interface limits. MODE is tied to separate RTN for current-limit/automatic retry. GND and RTN must remain isolated in copper. SHDN's internal pull-up enables cold startup; the motor ENN remains pulled high. Current limiting is nominal 0.50 A bootstrap and 1.00 A after a qualified PD contract, using two official 24 kΩ resistors and low-voltage-specified DMG1012T-7. The screened boot range is 0.4167–0.5867 A; the parallel range is 0.9314–1.0714 A. These are engineering screens; physical measurements are pending.

Proposed prototype limits: USB-C PD charger/powered dock advertising at least 1.5 A at initial 5 V; 9/12/15/20 V contracts, source no higher than 21 V, contract current at least 1.5 A before raising POWER_HIGH_CURRENT. Hold ENN high at 5 V, on disconnect or fault. Legacy/default-current USB hosts are not qualified. Target phase peak screen is 0.34245 A without credit for internal sense resistance. Limit speed to 300 RPM and external reflected inertia to 0.25×10⁻⁶ kg·m²; exclude continuous backdrive/overhauling. These are proposed design limits, not measured operating ratings.

At 28.8 mH maximum phase inductance, independently bounded phases plus rotor/load kinetic energy total 3.994 mJ. Two 100 µF Panasonic FP capacitors are screened at 100.8 µF combined after −20% initial tolerance, −10% reflow and −30% endurance allowances from the current manufacturer datasheet. Lossless VM rises from 21 V to 22.809 V. A conservative low-frequency tanδ/resistive-step screen gives 24.972 V. High-frequency parasitic overshoot is not included; short native power loops, appropriate planes and prototype measurements remain required. This is not a claim of transient certification or a brake for arbitrary loads.

The OVP divider screens 21.473–22.843 V including resistor temperature allowance, threshold limits and input leakage. C33 is 2.2 µF directly between dVdT and RTN. C34 is 2.2 µF at the protected input. Typical Taiyo Yuden bias/temperature charts, ±10% initial tolerance, 15% temperature and 20% aging allowances give a 1.330 µF dVdT screen and 0.186 µF input bypass at 21 V; these curves are typical rather than guaranteed combined limits. Maximum screened VM charging current is 28.5 mA. AP63203 soft start is 4 ms typical with no stated minimum; startup current and PD/firmware sequencing still require review.
