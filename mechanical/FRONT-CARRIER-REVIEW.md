# Front-flange carrier — revision 0.0.16-alpha.0

Unfabricated mechanical prototype for the exact STEPPERONLINE 14HM11-0404S. The manufacturer drawing and STEP are unchanged. Four front M3 holes on the documented 26 ±0.2 mm square attach the carrier. Motor rear structural screws remain untouched.

The 35 × 35 mm PCB has four Ø2.5 mm NPTH holes on a 30.5 mm square with all-layer 2.5 mm radius keepouts. The monolithic machined 6061-T6/T651 carrier has a 49.2 × 36.5 × 3 mm front plate, Ø24 mm pilot opening, Ø3.8 mm front bolt clearances, four direct 6 × 6 mm rails and Ø4.5 mm PCB support bosses. Motor rear/front datums are Z=-10/-38.2 mm; PCB midplane is Z=0 and support surface -0.8 mm. Returns end at -4.8 mm below the PCB. Proposed machining tolerance is ±0.05 mm.

The actual exported 111 component mesh envelopes clear the exact carrier and fastener solids by at least 1.000005 mm. There is no carrier/motor intersection. The fresh mounted rendering was inspected. The analysis includes fitted and DNP component bodies conservatively. Production component models, actual hardware installation and strength are not physically verified.

JLC04161H-3313 is ordered as 1.6 mm but the live manufacturer calculator displays 1.56 mm finished, ±10%. Calculations use minimum PCB thickness 1.404 mm, and include the 1.6 mm rendering offset in the clearance allowance. A single rail carries the entire declared 20 N cable/board load in the cantilever screen. Conservative combined rail/arm/PCB deflection is 0.1357 mm and section stress safety factor 10.81. FR4 modulus is an engineering allowance, not a laminate guarantee. This is not FEA, fatigue qualification or a physical load test.

Permitted hardware envelopes: M2×5 length 5 ±0.2 mm, head ≤Ø3.8 ×1.6 mm, custom washer Ø4.5/Ø2.2 ×0.50 ±0.05 mm; M3×6 head ≤Ø5.5 ×3 mm. These are dimensional procurement constraints, not a claimed purchased hardware model. Retention, preload, torque, insulation and physical installation remain Stage 7 tests.
