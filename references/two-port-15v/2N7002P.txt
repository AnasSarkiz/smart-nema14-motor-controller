1. Product profile

          1.1 General description
              N-channel enhancement mode Field-Effect Transistor (FET) in a small SOT23
              (TO-236AB) Surface-Mounted Device (SMD) plastic package using Trench MOSFET
              technology.

          1.2 Features and benefits
                AEC-Q101 qualified                                       Trench MOSFET technology
                Logic-level compatible                                   Very fast switching

          1.3 Applications
                High-speed line driver                                   Relay driver
                Low-side loadswitch                                      Switching circuits
